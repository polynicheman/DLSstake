How screenshot verification works. The app reads the two big team names on the scoreboard (the ones flanking the 90:00 clock — e.g. DHIBOSS and KRAFF), and the two scores between them. Bracket clan tags like [HPH] are ignored, and so are crest images, camera metadata, and GPS. Screenshots must be named Screenshot_YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS.jpg (e.g. Screenshot_20260923-100755.jpg) — the datetime embedded in the filename must fall within 60 minutes of the match window. If your score is higher, you take the pot. If scores are equal, the match settles as a draw and both stakes refund. Every settled match has an Explain button that shows exactly what was read.
